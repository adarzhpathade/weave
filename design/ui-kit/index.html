AS

First screens

390 px, the size of most phones. Example data: a 3rd-year CSIT-2 student in semester 5. Tap Menu on any phone, the subject cards, the topic circles and "Replay motion".

Motion

Playful but quick: shapes drift, cards spring, ticks pop with a little burst. Nothing slows the student down.

Screen change

View Transitions: the tapped card grows into the next screen. 360 ms, ease-out.

Lists

Items rise 18 px and fade in, 60 ms apart. Only on first load, never on every scroll.

Menu

The pill's button stretches into a full-screen menu (circle reveal, 640 ms). Items rise one after another; sub-options unfold.

Numbers

Countdowns tick every second; progress dials fill tick by tick when a note is saved.

Press

Buttons scale to 96% on press, 140 ms. Gives a native feel.

Reduced motion

If the phone asks for less motion, everything appears instantly and the badges stop spinning.
